Claude Plays a Puzzle Game

A little browser port of EZ Maze, playable right here. Use the arrow keys or WASD to move; the goal is to reach the key on each level. Some tiles move when you do — with you, against you, or perpendicular — so getting to the key is the puzzle.

Arrows / WASD move  ·  R restart  ·  P/Q next/prev level  ·  Enter start
